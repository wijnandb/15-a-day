# Smartcuts: The Breakthrough Power of Lateral Thinking by Shane Snow

## Summary
"Smartcuts: The Breakthrough Power of Lateral Thinking" by Shane Snow explores the concept of lateral thinking and how it can be harnessed to achieve breakthrough success faster than traditional methods. The book delves into historical and contemporary examples of how innovators, entrepreneurs, and leaders have utilized unconventional strategies to leap ahead in various fields. Snow introduces the idea of "smartcuts," a portmanteau of "smart" and "shortcuts," which are ethical and effective ways to accelerate progress by eschewing conventional steps and finding new paths to success.

## Review
Shane Snow's "Smartcuts" is an insightful exploration of how lateral thinking can dramatically change the way one approaches challenges and opportunities. The book is well-researched and combines engaging storytelling with practical advice, recognizing patterns that contribute to rapid success. One of its strengths is the diverse array of case studies that illustrate the power of looking beyond standard approaches. However, some readers might find that the depth of analysis could be more comprehensive, as the book primarily focuses on breadth rather than delving deeply into each example. Despite this, "Smartcuts" provides inspiration for anyone looking to rethink traditional methods of problem-solving and innovation.

## Key Takeaways
1. **Lateral Thinking:** Success often comes from thinking differently and finding innovative solutions, rather than following established paths.
2. **Momentum:** Building and maintaining momentum is crucial for rapid progress. This can be achieved through strategic breakthroughs that leverage past achievements.
3. **Platforms:** Utilize existing platforms to gain visibility and credibility rather than starting from scratch.
4. **Waves:** Identifying and riding industry trends or technological waves can accelerate success.
5. **10x Thinking:** Aim for exponential improvements instead of incremental changes to achieve significant breakthroughs.

## Recommendation
"Smartcuts" is recommended for entrepreneurs, innovators, and anyone interested in optimizing their problem-solving strategies. The book is particularly beneficial for those who are open to questioning conventional wisdom and are looking for inspiration to achieve faster, more efficient success in their endeavors.
